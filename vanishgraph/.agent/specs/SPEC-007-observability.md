# SPEC-007 Observability

OpenTelemetry traces, Prometheus-compatible metrics, and structured redacted logs share correlation IDs. Security and audit events are durable and unsampled. Crash evidence crosses DLP before persistence, issue creation, or model transport.

Required signals include workflow state, queue lag, recipe health, ambiguous effects, authority failures, cross-tenant denials, verification lag, reappearance, cost, and provider health. Readiness is false when required dependencies or migrations are unavailable.

Acceptance injects known failures and verifies truthful signals without secrets or PII.
