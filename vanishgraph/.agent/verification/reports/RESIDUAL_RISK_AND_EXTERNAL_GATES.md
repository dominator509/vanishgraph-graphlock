# Residual Risk and External Gates

- Legal/compliance: counsel must approve authority, jurisdiction policy, authorized-agent, minor/guardian, exemptions, and templates.
- Provider/vendor: current official auth, automation rights, entitlement, isolation, quota, and data-control evidence is required.
- Environment: staging cloud, KMS, object storage, managed Postgres, Temporal, and browser workers are not provisioned.
- Human: UAT and manual assistive-technology validation require named real validators.
- Security: independent penetration test and privacy threat-model/DPIA evidence are required before GA.
- Deployment: production deployment is manual and unauthorized in this epoch.

These are not passes. They constrain the release verdict to INCONCLUSIVE or NO_GO until resolved.
