    site cannot omit them. With no SDK, the plan's FALLBACK applies verbatim: the attributes are attached in ONE

    there is no second way to emit.

    pin three dependencies before any milestone has established what the metrics catalogue (§6), the alert catalogue (§8),

    from the decision, not before it. Later milestones that need a client take the ADR route then.
  * CONSEQUENCE, STATED SO IT IS NOT DISCOVERED LATER: OTLP trace export, the Prometheus scrape endpoint on GET /metrics,
    and the error-reporter POST are NOT implemented by M1 and are not claimed by it. M1 implements the identity layer and
